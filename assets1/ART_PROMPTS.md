# Dark fantasy game artwork

The initial seven PNG assets were generated with the built-in imagegen tool. No fallback generator was used. The build embeds these images into game.html.


## troll.png (transparent background)

Use case: stylized-concept. Transparent full-body enemy sprite for a dark fantasy dungeon game. Enormous hunched muscular moss-green cave troll, long arms, clawed hands, rough warty skin, tusks, ragged leather loincloth, gripping a massive crude stone club, threatening stance facing viewer. Highly detailed realistic painterly game art, muted earthy colors, warm torch rim light, entire figure feet and club inside frame with padding. No environment, no ground, no text, no UI. Genuine transparent background.

## troll_hall.png

Use case: stylized-concept. Wide 16:9 first-person dark fantasy dungeon background. A very large ancient vaulted crypt hall with rows of thick crumbling stone columns, carved gothic capitals, side aisles offering hiding places, damp flagstone floor, scattered roots and mist, tiny warm torches against charcoal green shadows. Detailed cinematic realistic painterly game environment, visible shadow detail, open central floor for composited enemy sprite, no creatures or people, no text or UI.


## dungeon.png

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: wide background for a first-person dark fantasy dungeon crawler. Create a richly detailed cinematic realistic digital painting, landscape 16:9. An intimate ancient stone crypt viewed at eye level, central gothic arched passage, damp uneven stone floor, wrought iron sconces glowing amber, ancient carvings, cobwebs, moss, mist and deep cool shadows. Moody mature dark fantasy, restrained ochre and charcoal palette, tactile crumbling masonry, painterly premium game environment. Empty foreground floor in lower half for composited enemies, no people, no monsters, no text, no UI, no logos. Architectural detail must remain visible, not crushed to black.

## vault_empty.png (edit of vault.png)

Edit this existing dark fantasy final treasure chamber image. Remove only the large glowing emerald seal gemstone from the center of the antique bronze ring holder. Keep the empty bronze ring holder, pedestal, all architecture, candles, atmosphere, framing, lighting, textures and every other element unchanged. This is the after-pickup state of a game background: the central holder must clearly be empty, and the removed gemstone should leave no green magical glow. No text, no UI.
